Phone PPG Hemodynamics Lab

Experimental browser-based PPG demonstrator using a cellphone camera and flash. It visualizes the pulse waveform and derives illustrative SpO₂, stroke-volume and cardiac-output estimates.
RESEARCH / DEMO — NOT FOR CLINICAL USE

Derived metrics

Heart rate—bpm
SpO₂*—%
Stroke volume*—mL/beat
Cardiac output*—L/min
Important: A phone camera PPG is not a validated pulse oximeter or hemodynamic monitor. SpO₂ requires calibrated multi-wavelength optical measurement, and stroke volume cannot be uniquely determined from PPG morphology alone. The values marked * are educational model estimates and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment, or emergency decisions.

Camera preview & signal acquisition

The browser samples average RGB intensity from the camera stream. Real devices differ substantially in camera spectral response, exposure control, flash behavior and image processing.
