Interpretation and limitations

SpO₂ is an experimental camera estimate. Standard pulse oximetry uses differential optical absorption, typically including red and near-infrared wavelengths; ordinary phone cameras do not provide a standardized IR channel.
Stroke volume is not uniquely determined by PPG morphology. The displayed SV/CO is a transparent, calibration-capable research model scaffold using waveform features plus entered BP/body variables, not a validated hemodynamic measurement.
The pipeline performs detrending, approximate band-pass filtering, adaptive peak detection, dicrotic-notch/diastolic-peak detection, signal-quality scoring and RGB feature extraction. Use ECG for HR validation, a validated oximeter/arterial reference for SpO₂, and an accepted hemodynamic reference for SV/CO validation.
